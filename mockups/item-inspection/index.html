Item inspection — layout studies

Seven treatments of the same record (EPH-2026-003, Eiffel Tower Ticket, 130 × 54 mm), ordered from standardized to experimental. All reuse the site's tokens: vellum on black, EB Garamond, Commit Mono, hairline rules.

Standardized
01 Catalog card — calibrated plate 325 mm field Ruled label/value fields plus a measured 0–325 mm plate: scales on top and left, item at the origin at true proportion. Oversize items rescale the field with a declared ratio. Implemented as the production default — see docs/catalog-card-analysis.md. 02 Specimen plate mm rulers Item isolated center-field with calibrated mm rulers along bottom and left edges; figure caption beneath. Switch ticket/LP top-right. 03 Finding aid / ledger text dims Text-forward register with dotted leaders; the image is a small evidence cell. Borrows the biography/CV box border language.
Scale system
04 True-scale frame (305 mm) 3 oversize modes A constant LP-sized frame; every item at real proportion. Pick the poster, then compare ratio / overflow / ghost treatments.
Experimental
05 Light table archivist's slip Item on an illuminated surface; metadata on a pinned paper slip; mm strip etched into the table edge. 06 Microfiche frame edge codes Film cell with sprockets and burned-in edge codes; adjacent series items as ghost frames. Dimensions live in the bottom-right edge code. 07 Exploded annotations dim callouts Metadata pinned around the item with hairlines; technical- drawing extension lines carry the measurements.
