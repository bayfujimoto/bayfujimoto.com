Three real records (CONTACT-2026-001 … 003: email, Instagram, Letterboxd). Today they open in the generic card with no channel shown at all. The studies agree on one thing — the plate carries a card at true scale on the ordinary mm plate, so the calibrated grammar is honest again (a calling card is 89 × 51 mm) — and differ on the structure and the object.

idea A — one record, no strip

01A calling card at true scale The three files fold into one record with a channels list (as the template intends). 02Calling cards, one per channel The frames contract as the Guide and CV use it: three records, a shared strip, ↑/↓ to step. Each plate is a card printed for that one channel; the fields column rebuilds per frame. Typographic tiles.

idea C — a card file

03Index cards with tabs Same structure as 02, a different object: each channel typed on a ruled 127 × 76 mm index card with a tab; the strip is the row of tabs. The archivist's own address file, not a card he hands out.
