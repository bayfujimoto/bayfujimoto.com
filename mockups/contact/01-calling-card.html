Idea A. Contact is one record (the three files fold into one channels list, as the template intends). The plate is the ordinary calibrated plate: an 89 × 51 mm calling card at the origin, opened at fit zoom, flip for the verso. The card is typeset from the record — or, if Bay scans his real card, the scan takes the plate (see). Every channel is a row and a live link.
