short_bio: "Bay Fujimoto is a designer and architecture student based in Houston, Texas. He is completing a Bachelor of Architecture at Rice University."
long_bio: "His work sits at the intersection of architectural history, design research, and material culture. He is interested in how buildings and objects are used, accumulated, and remembered — the ways in which ordinary things carry traces of the lives they have been part of.\n\nAt Rice University he has studied the formal logic of buildings as objects, the history of modernism's institutional forms, and the relationship between design and everyday life. A semester abroad in Paris through the Rice School of Architecture deepened his interest in archival research as a design method.\n\nAlongside academic work he has taken on design and research projects across professional and independent contexts. He is drawn to work that is specific rather than universal, that takes the particular conditions of a problem seriously rather than defaulting to generic solutions.\n\nThis site is an ongoing attempt to keep a record of that work — not as a portfolio, but as an archive."
